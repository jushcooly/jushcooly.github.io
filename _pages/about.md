I am a senior AI undergraduate at Yonsei University with hands-on research experience in machine learning. My interest centers on reinforcement learning for physical, embodied systems, and on learning-based methods grounded in physical and geometric structure. Having navigated physical mobility challenges firsthand, I bring a grounded, physical-therapy-informed perspective on movement and embodiment that I aim to carry into research on physical AI.
<h2>Interested in</h2>

**Reinforcement Learning** - I am drawn to how reinforcement learning lets models discover novel, creative behaviors through the way they are trained. I am especially interested in applying it to physical, embodied systems — learning-based methods grounded in physical and geometric structure for agents that act reliably in the real world.
